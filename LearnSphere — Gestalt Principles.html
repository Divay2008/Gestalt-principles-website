Design process

How the LearnSphere experience was planned and structured.

  1. Understand the problem: Learners need one clear place to discover courses and monitor progress.
  2. Identify users: University students and independent learners who value simple, self-paced learning.
  3. Plan the structure: Overview, Explore Courses, My Learning, Gestalt Principles and Design Process.
  4. Apply visual principles: Proximity, similarity, continuity, closure, figure–ground, common region, common fate and focal point — switch on the Gestalt lens to see each one.
  5. Build and review: Check navigation, search, enrolment, progress updates, keyboard focus and responsive layout.

Testing reminder: Ask real users to search, enrol and find their progress. Record their actual feedback and the improvements you made. This demo uses sample courses and browser storage; it is not connected to a live student database.
